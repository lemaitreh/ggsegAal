# ggsegAAL: Automated Anatomical Labeling atlases

The ggsegAAL package provides AAL1, AAL2 and AAL3 representations for
the ggseg ecosystem.

## Details

The atlas family is divided into cortical, subcortical and cerebellar
components.

## Atlas composition

- **AAL1:** 82 cortical + 8 subcortical + 26 cerebellar = **116
  regions**.

- **AAL2:** 86 cortical, including the AAL2 orbitofrontal
  parcellation, + 8 subcortical + 26 cerebellar = **120 regions**.

- **AAL3:** 90 cortical, including anterior cingulate subdivisions, + 50
  subcortical, including thalamic subdivisions, + 26 cerebellar = **166
  regions**.

## Representations

Cortical atlases are represented on the Colin27 cortical surface.

Subcortical atlases include a contextual cortical representation for 2D
plotting.

Cerebellar atlases are transformed to SUIT space and provide flatmap and
3D representations.
